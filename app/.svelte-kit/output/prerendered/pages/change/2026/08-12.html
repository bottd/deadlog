Riposte change history

Riposte

  • Riposte melee resist reduction increased from -22% to -25%
  • Riposte T2 changed from "-30% Melee Resist" to "-25% Melee Resist and +0.4s Stun Duration"
  • Riposte T3 increased from 60% Lifesteal for 7s to 75% Lifesteal for 13s
Current details for Riposte · client 6698

Prepare to deflect the next incoming attack. On a successful deflection, briefly become invulnerable and target an enemy hero to dash towards them, stunning them and reducing their Melee Resist.

Press Ability 2 to select a target.

Does not trigger against trooper or neutral damage.

Tier 2
-25% Melee Resist and +0.4s Stun Duration
Tier 3
Gain 75% Bullet, Spirit & Melee Lifesteal against the Riposte target for 13s

Base values, before upgrades and scaling

Deflect Duration0.8s
Invulnerability Duration0.3s
Stun Duration0.8s
Melee Resist-25%
Melee Resist Reduction Duration3s
